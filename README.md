# TWDP Sales Performance Dashboard | Excel Data Analytics Project

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing sales performance using Microsoft Excel to evaluate revenue, profitability, product performance, regional sales distribution, and payment methods.

The analysis covers five months of sales activity, from January to May 2026, using 49 sales orders across seven cities and ten sales representatives.

An interactive Excel dashboard was developed to transform raw sales records into meaningful business insights, monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify revenue trends, and support data-driven business decisions.

## Business Problem

Businesses need to understand how their sales activities contribute to overall financial performance. Monitoring revenue alone is not sufficient; understanding product performance, regional contributions, payment preferences, and sales representative performance is equally important.

This project addresses the following business question:

**How can sales data be analysed and visualised to evaluate business performance, identify revenue drivers, and generate actionable insights that support better business decisions?**

## Project Objectives

The objectives of this project are to:

The project followed a structured data analysis workflow:

1. **Data Inspection:** Examined the sales dataset to understand its structure, variables, and business context.
2. **Data Preparation:** Organised the sales records and prepared the relevant fields for analysis.
3. **Data Cleaning:** Reviewed the dataset and applied data-quality checks.
4. **Data Transformation:** Created calculated fields to support financial and sales performance analysis.
5. **Data Analysis:** Used PivotTables and Excel calculations to summarise sales performance across different business dimensions.
6. **KPI Development:** Established key performance indicators to measure revenue, profit, orders, and units sold.
7. **Dashboard Development:** Created visual representations of the analytical results.
8. **Business Insights:** Interpreted the findings and developed recommendations based on observed sales patterns.

## Key Business Insights

### 1. Overall Sales Performance

The business generated approximately ₦43.3 million in revenue from 49 orders, with a total profit of approximately ₦9.4 million.

The recorded profit margin was approximately 21.66%, providing an overview of the business's profitability during the reporting period.

### 2. Electronics Drives Revenue

Electronics emerged as the highest revenue-generating category, contributing approximately ₦23.78 million.

Furniture generated ₦10.20 million, while Accessories contributed ₦9.27 million.

This indicates that Electronics represents the largest revenue contributor among the analysed product categories.

### 3. Lagos Leads Regional Sales

Lagos recorded the highest revenue contribution of approximately ₦14.6 million, followed by Abuja with approximately ₦8.8 million.

This highlights the importance of these locations to overall sales performance.

### 4. Laptops Are the Highest-Revenue Product

The top five products by revenue were:

| Product | Revenue |
|---|---:|
| Laptop | ₦11.72M |
| Monitor | ₦8.11M |
| Office Chair | ₦5.45M |
| Desk | ₦4.75M |
| Printer | ₦3.95M |

Laptops and Monitors were the leading individual product contributors to revenue.

### 5. May Recorded the Highest Monthly Revenue

May generated approximately ₦10.08 million, making it the highest-revenue month during the reporting period.

Revenue increased from January through March, declined in April, and reached its highest point in May.

### 6. Payment Method Performance

Card payments recorded the highest revenue contribution, followed by bank transfers and cash payments.

This provides an overview of the payment channels represented in the sales dataset.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analytical findings, the following recommendations are proposed:

1. **Inventory Planning:** Prioritise inventory monitoring for high-revenue products, particularly laptops and monitors, while considering actual demand and stock availability.

2. **Regional Sales Strategy:** Investigate opportunities to strengthen sales performance in lower-revenue locations while maintaining strong market coverage in Lagos and Abuja.

3. **Product Performance Monitoring:** Regularly evaluate product-level revenue to identify changing demand patterns and support better inventory decisions.

4. **Sales Performance Evaluation:** Monitor sales representative contributions to identify performance trends and opportunities for improved sales strategies.

5. **Payment Monitoring:** Maintain accurate payment records and monitor payment method distribution to support reliable financial reporting.

6. **Continuous Performance Reporting:** Use regular dashboard reviews to monitor revenue, profitability, and sales trends and support informed business decisions.
